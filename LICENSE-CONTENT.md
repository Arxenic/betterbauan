## What to credit

Credit **BetterBauan** for the compilation, and credit the agency that produced the underlying record:

- Population, households, land area: Philippine Statistics Authority, 2024 Census of Population.
- Financial and statutory filings: Department of the Interior and Local Government, Full Disclosure Policy
  Portal (the documents remain the municipality's).
- Infrastructure contracts: Department of Public Works and Highways.

Government records themselves are public documents; this licence covers the selection, arrangement and
annotation produced by this project.
